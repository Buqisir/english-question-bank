## What to build

Complete the second half of the public Format 2 Import Job. An exact Teacher
Confirmation resumes the same durable Job, invokes the existing Canonical
compiler and atomic publisher, proves an exact committed local reload, and
returns an `awaiting_qa` result with prepared Screen, Student Print, Teacher
Print, and PDF entry points. The compact synthetic fixture becomes a complete
end-to-end proof from admitted sources to local consumption.

## Allowed and forbidden scope

- Allowed: the Job resume operation, downstream stage invalidation,
  idempotent local publication/recovery, QA handoff projection, and public-seam
  tests for those behaviors.
- Reuse the accepted Canonical compiler, public publisher, recovery, consumer,
  PaperSurface, and Print/PDF seams; the Job may orchestrate but not reconstruct
  their meaning.
- Forbidden: production publication, QA acceptance on the Owner's behalf,
  renderer forks, paper-specific code, website import UI, or a second
  publication implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The public resume operation accepts the same Job identity plus an exact confirmation bound to the complete candidate-package digest; stale, partial, rejected, or substituted confirmation leaves the Job stopped with zero publication.
- [ ] Exact confirmation produces one complete Canonical Paper through the existing compiler or a precise unresolved result; the coordinator never hand-authors Canonical groups, media closure, paper plans, or publication manifests.
- [ ] Local publication is one whole-paper atomic operation and success requires exact committed reload, not process exit or a success string.
- [ ] An interrupted publish recovers and retries idempotently; an incompatible immutable publication fails without overwrite, deletion, or mixed-version state.
- [ ] Repeating an unchanged completed Job returns the exact committed identities as a proven no-op.
- [ ] Changing source, transcription, candidate bytes, dimensions, layout evidence, confirmation, or authority invalidates every dependent result and cannot retain `awaiting_qa`.
- [ ] Screen, Student Print, Teacher Print, and PDF consume the same Canonical Paper and confirmed media/layout intent; Student output contains no answer or Analysis leakage.
- [ ] The local console reports `awaiting_qa` and prepares exact service, paperId, URLs, first focus state, print views, PDF, and one concise gate summary without writing the Owner QA receipt.
- [ ] The Job stops after local QA preparation and performs no production, R2, deploy, ship, or external mutation.
- [ ] Focused public-Job, compiler, publisher recovery, exact reload, shared consumer, and `git diff --check` checks pass. The final full repository gate remains Ticket 06 work.
