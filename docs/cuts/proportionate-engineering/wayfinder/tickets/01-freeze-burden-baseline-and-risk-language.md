## Question

### Hypothesis and evidence target

The preliminary counts mix drifting HEADs and ambiguous denominators, and the
repository lacks one reproducible vocabulary tying each common workflow to its
blast radius, irreversibility, automation authority, and named failures.
Produce a source-HEAD-bound inventory recipe and four workflow traces: ordinary
Canonical Paper Data Addition, UI micro-change, Shared Contract Change, and
high-risk persistence/auth change.

### Affected files and surfaces

Read all tracked files; count `scripts/verify-*.mjs`, canonical standards and
schemas, cut tickets, manifest versions, consumer artifacts, current CI/package
entrypoints, and the four workflows' present required actions. Write only this
ticket's resolution and one linked research asset under
`docs/cuts/proportionate-engineering/research/`.

### Exact decision to return

Which denominators are reproducible at the exact source HEAD, what canonical
definitions distinguish Data Addition, Shared Contract Change, UI Micro-change,
Atomic Publication, Risk Tier, and Risk Closure, and what is the measured
before-state action count for each workflow without inventing time, cost, quota,
or model-quality data?

### Classification candidates

Keep, downgrade, or delete each proposed metric; split ambiguous inventories by
denominator rather than merging incomparable counts.

### Named failure and deletion test

Prevent false reform conclusions caused by stale counts or category mixing.
Delete any metric that cannot be reproduced from named paths and a command, or
whose removal does not change a later keep/downgrade/delete decision.

### Risk tier and why

P0 planning authority: wrong denominators contaminate every later deletion and
could weaken real safety or preserve needless burden.
