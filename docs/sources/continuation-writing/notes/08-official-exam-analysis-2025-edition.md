Source: 教育部教育考试院编，中国教育出版传媒集团 / 语文出版社，
2024. The supplied PDF is the official 2025-edition analysis of the 2024
national papers.

Relevant PDF pages: 7–10 (printed pp. 1–4, overall evaluation) and 156–160

The entire 228-page PDF was subsequently OCR-indexed to verify that no other

## Authority and artifact boundary

This is official annual examination analysis and therefore outranks research
and classroom interpretations for the particular task. The supplied PDF gives
the task, construct explanation, detailed permissible content reasoning, and
quality criteria. It does **not** contain an English model continuation for
this task. Its appended 2023 national-paper answer section explicitly omits the
writing answers (`略`, PDF pp. 223–228). “Possible content” in the analysis
must not be relabeled as the one official answer.

## Overall official positioning

The general evaluation describes the task as integrated `reading + writing`
and as open enough to assess independent thinking, imagination, logical
reasoning, innovation, and comprehensive language use (PDF pp. 8–10). The
Gunter taxi story is also framed around trust, keeping a promise, and integrity;
these values emerge from the source conflict rather than from a detachable
moral ending.

## Task-specific construct

The task asks the writer to read the material, reasonably create subsequent
content, and express it accurately and coherently with appropriate vocabulary
and grammar. The official analysis requires logical connection to the source,
reasonable development, and a complete structure (PDF pp. 157–158).

The closing rubric explanation uses the current three-part wording:

1. created-content quality, completeness, and fit with the source situation;
2. vocabulary/grammar accuracy, appropriateness, and diversity;
3. cohesion across context and coherence of the whole text (PDF pp. 159–160).

This confirms S01's current wording with official provenance.

## Constraint reasoning in the Gunter task

The source ending establishes an urgent conflict: the narrator cannot pay the
taxi fare, the last bus is about to depart, and the available cash machine is
broken. Starter one requires the narrator to return and report the bad news.
Starter two says that four days later the narrator calls Gunter **as promised**.

The official analysis reasons backward from starter two:

- paragraph one must plausibly let the narrator catch the bus;
- a promise to settle the fare later must be established;
- because a later call occurs, paragraph one must also establish a means of
  contact;
- Gunter's acceptance entails trust and risk, which resolves the immediate
  conflict while licensing the later value development (PDF pp. 158–159).

These are boundary-state necessities, not merely plot preferences.

## Official openness: more than one valid result

The analysis offers several possible second-paragraph developments. The
narrator may contact Gunter, pay the fare, and express gratitude; an unexpected
event may prevent successful repayment, leaving regret while preserving the
memory of Gunter's help; other developments are allowed if they connect,
respect all source facts, close appropriately, and yield a complete text
(PDF p. 159).

This is direct official evidence against treating a reference continuation as
an answer key. Required states and open realization space must be represented
separately.

## Later field-design pressure (not a field decision)

- Store exact source facts, unresolved conflict, starter-one obligations,
  starter-two back-propagated prerequisites, and final closure separately.
- Make each “must” traceable to source/starter evidence; label the official
  book's alternatives as examples, not required events.
- Represent value/theme as developed through decisions and consequences, not
  as a mandatory concluding slogan.
- Keep holistic quality criteria distinct from the task-specific constraint
  graph.
