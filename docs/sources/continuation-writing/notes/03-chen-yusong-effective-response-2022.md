# S03 — 陈玉松：从评分标准角度谈读后续写的有效应答

Source: 陈玉松，江苏省锡东高级中学，《中小学外语教学（中学篇）》
2022 年第 7 期，第 15–20 页.

## Source role

This is a teacher's systematic interpretation of the then-current rubric,
demonstrated through the 2021 New Gaokao I “Mother's Day breakfast” narrative.
It is valuable for operational reasoning, but it must not override newer
rubric wording or be generalized from one narrative to all discourse types.

## Rubric interpretation

The paper groups effective response under writing content, discourse structure,
and language use. It reproduces the older fifth/fourth-band rubric, including
the now-retired marked-keyword requirement, and explicitly notes that newer
tasks had already removed marked keywords (pp. 15–16).

Its four practical response properties are content continuity, language
richness, discourse coherence, and writing neatness. The first three are
semantic/communicative properties; word count, spelling, punctuation, and
legibility are separate conventions/penalties (p. 16).

## Content reasoning in the worked example

The paper uses a narrative arc and character-emotion trajectory to reconstruct
the source, identify its conflict, and project a resolution (pp. 16–17). It
then asks the writer to infer theme from the complete source rather than attach
an unrelated ending.

The strongest reusable method is not the Freytag diagram itself but the
constraint reasoning around paragraph starters:

- the first starter must follow from the source ending;
- the second starter constrains what the first paragraph must have achieved;
- each paragraph must supply the state transition needed by the next boundary;
- a question chain can expose missing causal links before drafting (pp. 17–18).

For the example, the second starter says the twins carry breakfast upstairs;
therefore the first paragraph must plausibly transform a failed breakfast into
a prepared one. This is backward constraint propagation, not a prescribed plot
template.

The paper distinguishes three relations that must remain logical: continuation
to source, paragraph one to paragraph two, and continuation to each provided
starter (p. 18). It also recommends recovering foreshadowing and other hidden
source information so later events answer earlier setup.

## Language and style alignment

The source text is analyzed before recommending language. In this example its
style is predominantly plain narration, varied sentence patterns, detailed
action, and content-bearing verbs/nouns/adjectives/adverbs (pp. 18–19).
Consequently, the proposed continuation remains mainly plain narrative, mixes
simple and complex structures, and uses limited direct speech because the
source itself contains no dialogue.

This source-responsive qualification matters more than any individual device.
“Show, don't tell”, action detail, sensory detail, and emotional language are
presented as useful narrative techniques, but they remain means selected for
this source and genre rather than universal scoring dimensions (pp. 19–20).

## Useful operational distinctions

- source facts and clues versus inferred future events;
- theme inferred from the whole source versus explicit “theme elevation” in a
  final sentence;
- logical event sufficiency versus decorative detail;
- local cohesive devices versus global causal/structural coherence;
- source-style alignment versus conspicuous sentence complexity;
- an evidence-driven question chain versus a memorized plot frame.

## Cautions for later synthesis

1. Freytag's pyramid is a reading aid for a narrative case, not an official
   scoring requirement.
2. A five-W-one-H summary, emotional curve, word bank, and “show, don't tell”
   are classroom strategies, not universal analysis fields.
3. The sample continuation is one valid realization, not the answer key.
4. The paper's old rubric evidence must be versioned; marked-keyword coverage
   cannot leak into the current schema.
5. Counting connectors or complex sentences cannot substitute for explaining
   the relation they realize.

## Later field-design pressure (not a field decision)

A useful analysis may need explicit boundary constraints and state transitions:
source ending → paragraph-one starter → paragraph-one ending → paragraph-two
starter → final state. It should also be able to connect a proposed event to
the exact source clue or starter that licenses it, while keeping genre-specific
plot/emotion/description analysis conditional.
