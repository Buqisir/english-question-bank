# S10–S11 curriculum crosswalk

This crosswalk combines the two official standards only far enough to preserve
their different roles. It is not the final analysis-field design.

| Question | 2020 revision | 2025 revision | Working interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Is continuation in the curriculum/assessment family? | Explicitly names continuation in integrated reading work, level-2 quality, exam forms, and story continuation | Does not name the task | Yes; S11 establishes the explicit link, while newer official task analysis proves continued use |
| Is continuation necessarily a story? | Gives story continuation as one illustrative writing form | Requires multiple discourse types and different written tasks | No; type and communicative purpose are task-specific |
| What is the Gaokao quality ceiling? | Level 2 is the main basis | Gaokao does not exceed level 2 | Level-3 complexity cannot become a hidden requirement |
| What must reading recover? | information, implicit meaning, new/old information, semantic/logical order | type, structure, language, purpose, relation, attitude, author strategy | Build a source model richer than plot summary |
| What must writing accomplish? | describe, inform, argue, express emotion, explain, continue; choose vocabulary/grammar; cohere | describe, explain, interpret, argue, evaluate; organize for purpose/audience; communicate clearly, appropriately, coherently | Shared core is purpose-bound meaning construction, not narrative decoration |
| How should language be judged? | fluency, accuracy, appropriateness; purposeful selection | form–meaning–use, context, pragmatics, accuracy/appropriateness, discourse fluency | Interpret forms by communicative function; avoid raw feature counts |
| How open is the answer? | Diverse forms and comprehensive evaluation | authentic/open tasks, acceptable alternative answers, performance-based scoring | Separate hard constraints from legitimate realization space |

## Stable official core exposed by both

- receptive and productive skills operate together;
- the writer must understand source meaning and relations before creating;
- the created text has a purpose, structure, and audience/context;
- vocabulary, grammar, cohesion, and discourse organization serve meaning;
- logical relation, completeness, and effective/appropriate expression matter;
- thinking and culture are embedded in interpretation and communication rather
  than appended as slogans.

## Explicit non-decisions

This crosswalk does not yet decide JSON keys, nesting, scoring weights,
enumerations, required/optional fields, or model-output format. Those choices
must wait until the complete source set has passed coverage review.
