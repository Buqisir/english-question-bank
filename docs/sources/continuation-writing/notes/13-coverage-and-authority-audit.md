## Fixed evidence conclusions before field design

The source pass establishes only these constraints:

- the task is integrated reading, constrained creation, and written expression;
- the published source and both starters define hard and soft continuation
  constraints;
- current quality judgment is holistic across content/completeness/source fit,
  language accuracy/appropriateness/diversity, and cohesion/coherence;
- discourse purpose/type, audience, pragmatic effect, and source-language
  alignment change what good realization looks like;
- narrative, expository, argumentative, and mixed continuations must share a
  core without being forced through the same detail template;
- official examples define permissible reasoning space, not a single answer;
- any future schema must explain its evidence and version its authorities.
