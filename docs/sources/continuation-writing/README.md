# Continuation-writing research notes

Product contract (now on `main`):
`docs/product/continuation-writing-analysis-contract-v1.md`.

This directory records source-by-source research that grounded that contract.
The notes were written source-first; they are evidence, not a second contract.

## Evidence policy

The same statement can mean different things depending on its source. Keep the
following layers separate:

1. the task text, paragraph starters, and any official rubric distributed with
   the task;
2. official curriculum standards and official annual examination-analysis
   books;
3. papers by task designers or the national examination authority;
4. peer-reviewed research and teaching interpretations;
5. an unattributed or non-official screenshot, which can show current wording
   but cannot establish provenance by itself.

The 2025 curriculum standard is the current general writing authority. The
2020 standard is older, but it explicitly names continuation writing; neither
may silently replace the other. Likewise, a model answer demonstrates one
acceptable realization, not the only valid continuation.

## Note rules

- Record what the source actually supports, including page or section evidence.
- Separate official constructs from teacher operationalizations and classroom
  techniques.
- Record negative evidence and cautions, especially mechanical templates,
  memorized description, and narrative-only assumptions.
- Do not turn one example story into a universal field.
- Do not copy full articles or passages into the repository. These are analytic
  notes, not substitute editions of the source material.

## Reading status

The source register and individual notes live in [`notes/`](notes/). “Read”
means the complete relevant source was inspected; “OCR reviewed” means the
recognition output was checked against the page image where layout or wording
matters.

The later, owner-scoped narrative-only field proposal is kept separately at
[`../../product/continuation-writing-analysis-contract-v1.md`](../../product/continuation-writing-analysis-contract-v1.md).
That proposal may synthesize these notes, but it does not retroactively change
what any individual source supports.
