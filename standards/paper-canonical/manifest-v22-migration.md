# Manifest v22 migration

Manifest v22 succeeds, but does not rewrite, Manifest v21. The successor adds
the Grammar Fill public-geometry-fixtures v1 authority, its exact schema and
conformance fixture. The authority publishes only source-bound em widths and
producer-owned page-break identities selected before Screen, Print HTML, or
PDF production; generated public output is measured unchanged.

Grammar Fill rhythm v2, its source/golden/coverage oracles, every family
contract, and all earlier manifests and consumers remain byte-for-byte resolvable.
Screen v22 and Print/PDF v23 consume the fixture authority while
ordinary production callers omit the optional fixture selection.
Normalization Report v22 and Publication v22 close the new manifest from
import through publication and historical loading. Generated DOCX stays
retired.
