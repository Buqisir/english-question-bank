authority closure. Screen v24 and Print/PDF v25 are the only current consumers.
Manifest v1-v23 and every artifact they reference remain byte-for-byte resolvable.

Grammar Fill family v2 owns ordered prompt bindings. Source Adapter v6 admits
them only from one accepted, source-digest-bound prompt capture and records an
exact source observation for either `source_prompt` or
`source_proven_none`. Core Invariants v2 selects Question Group v2, whose Blank
references remain identity-only; renderer geometry cannot become a shadow
prompt source.

Grammar Fill rhythm v4 and public-geometry fixture authority v3 contain no
wrapped-line px/pt shadow geometry. Semantic authority requires the exact
fixture artifact and schema. Fixture resolution also validates the ready
Consumption Plan through the ordinary occurrence-integrity seam and requires
the exact surface Consumer Contract; absent, historical, refs-only, missing,
or mismatched Plans fail closed before Screen, Print HTML, or PDF production.

This migration adds no generated DOCX route and changes no production or R2
state.
