# Manifest v50 migration

Manifest v50 succeeds immutable Candidate-only Manifest v49
(`sha256:d9a640d5477d271e14b6a541d5b915fa28216308bbf50ff89ad3585b694001bf`)
without modifying predecessor bytes. It extends only Universal NCK1 import and
Owner Review: NCK1 Profile v15 binds source-backed Listening Questions 1–5 and
6–20 to two optional Canonical `listening` Groups under Listening family v1.
When the source explicitly records `not_supplied`, both slots are omitted and
produce no visible chrome or phantom spacing.

Captured Listening preserves A–C options, official answers, transcript segment
coverage, and exact Source Map anchors. Review Screen places the two Groups
before Reading and uses the existing selected-question right rail for the
official transcript, answer, and compact audio-review evidence. Student
projection excludes transcripts, official answers, and audio-review evidence.

Manifest v50 does not advance globally accepted Manifest v36, add a separate
Listening approval workflow, or claim public audio delivery and Print/PDF
Listening parity. Publications carrying Listening audio remain rejected until
those shared output contracts are introduced by a later successor. Manifest
v49 remains registered and resolvable for immutable historical Candidates and
Review revisions.
