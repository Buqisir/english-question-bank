does not change canonical passage text, Blank identity or source order,
prompt identity, official answers, reviewed analysis, family contracts, or
any previously reviewed Reading, Seven Choices, and Cloze authority.

digest-pinned Grammar Fill reference image. It owns the complete semantic-role
adjacency graph, normalized vertical and horizontal strategies, 3.3em-class
blank geometry, prompt attachment, Screen and exact-A4 Print/PDF targets, and
their tolerances. It also publishes the exact 2026-I Group, paragraph,
Question, Blank, prompt, answer, source-asset, source-PDF, official-answer,
reviewed-analysis, and baseline/adversarial occurrence identities. Screen v20
and Print/PDF v21 consume the same canonical projection and require
per-occurrence Grammar Fill integrity digests.

renderer mapping. The Print producer reopens the immutable Publication Source
Capture and official-answer artifact, verifies the reviewed-analysis digest,
and binds these bytes to the exact projected occurrence before writing public
HTML. Missing or drifted source text, Question/Blank order, prompt,
prompt-attachment geometry, answer, explanation, authority, or integrity
envelope therefore fails closed with zero artifact.

Manifest v1-v19, the exact historical v13 projection, every earlier Consumer
Contract, schema, Publication, source byte, official-answer byte, analysis
byte, and evidence artifact remains immutable and resolvable. Generated DOCX
stays retired.
