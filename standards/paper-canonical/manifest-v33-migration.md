# Manifest v33 migration

Manifest v33 succeeds v32 without modifying any predecessor bytes.

It replaces only the current Paper Typography, Cloze Rhythm, Grammar Fill
Rhythm, Grammar Fill public-geometry fixture, normalization-report, and
canonical-publication descriptors with their versioned successors. Screen v33
and Print/PDF v34 bind the new manifest. Manifest v1 through v32 and every
authority, schema, fixture, consumer, and migration they reference remain unchanged and resolvable.
