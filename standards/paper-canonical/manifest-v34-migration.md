# Manifest v34 migration

Manifest v34 succeeds v33 without modifying any predecessor bytes.

It replaces only the current Paper Typography, Continuation Writing Rhythm, and
the compatible Grammar Fill public-geometry fixture, plus the
normalization-report and canonical-publication descriptors required by the
manifest successor. The Continuation successor adds the exact-final-HEAD
public-artifact ledger oracle; Paper Typography v9 owns section-heading and
instruction regular weights. The v13 Grammar Fill fixture advances only its
compatible manifest identity. Screen v34 and Print/PDF v35 bind the new
manifest. Manifest v1 through v33 and every authority, schema, fixture,
consumer, and migration they reference remain unchanged and resolvable.
