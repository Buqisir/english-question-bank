# Manifest v36 migration

Manifest v36 succeeds v35 without modifying any predecessor bytes. It adds
the versioned `docx-source-envelope` adapter and its fail-closed envelope
schema, then advances the normalization-report and canonical-publication
schema identities to v36. The unchanged Grammar Fill public geometry evidence
is versioned as v15 solely to bind its compatible Consumption Plan to Manifest
v36. No profile, family, geometry measurement, or normalization rule changes.
Screen v36 and Print/PDF v37 bind Manifest v36. Earlier manifests,
schemas, adapters, consumer contracts, and migrations remain resolvable.
