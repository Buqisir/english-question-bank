# Manifest v15 migration

Manifest v15 adds one renderer-owned Seven Choices Golden Geometry artifact
without changing canonical paper semantics. The new artifact binds the exact
golden page-08 JPEG bytes, an explicit semantic-role adjacency graph, and only
dimensionless local-baseline, local-em, and usable-content-width ratios for
Screen and exact-A4 Print/PDF.

The vertical axis covers optional chrome collapse, passage and paragraph
rhythm, inline blank markers, A-G bank entry and option transitions,
Student/Teacher tails, following sections, and same/cross-page continuation.
The horizontal axis classifies the golden A-G bank from repeated coordinate
clusters: one full remaining-width row track, a fixed label-origin cluster,
content-driven text starts after each label glyph plus a fixed em gap,
content-driven right bounds, no distributed or space-between placement, and
hanging-indent continuation. The golden contains only one-line options, so
wrapped continuation is fixture-proved and is not presented as a golden
observation.

The reusable Golden Geometry Framework records these vertical and horizontal
axes for later bounded families, but explicitly shares no family values or
layout strategy. Raw JPEG coordinates never become CSS pixels or PDF points,
and no scale is inferred from the full A4 page.

Renderer Typography v4 remains byte-identical and continues to own the
already-closed Reading A-D rhythm. The Seven Choices contract depends on that
Reading-D boundary only by its exact published digest and never derives Seven
Choices scale from Reading pages.

New canonical publications bind Screen v15 and Print/PDF v16. Manifest v1-v14,
Renderer Typography v1-v4, their consumer contracts, and all existing
Publications remain immutable parse-only authority.

Admission, canonical content semantics, blank identity/order, A-G bank text
and order, answers, Analysis, rich-content order, horizontal placement, and
non-Seven-Choices rendering do not change.
