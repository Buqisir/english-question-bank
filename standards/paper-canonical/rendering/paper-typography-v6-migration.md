# Paper Typography v6 migration

Paper Typography v6 preserves v5 Reading A-D identity, fixed 18pt baseline,
and all existing family geometry. It adds one Owner-gold section-heading and
horizontal-anchor contract for all six current surfaces.

Part and section headings share the body-left edge. Section headings have zero
indent and use `"SimSun", "Songti SC", "STSong", serif` for Chinese, digits,
and decimal points on one baseline. Group instructions and passage first
lines begin at 2em; wrapped passage lines return to the body-left edge. The
contract binds the three retained Owner screenshots by file digest.
