# Continuation Writing rhythm v3 migration

v3 preserves the complete v2 role graph, two distinct 4em supplied starters,
145mm × 57mm normal-flow response geometry, evidence digests, and fixed 18pt
Print/PDF baseline. It replaces `borderPt: 0.48` with
`screenBorderPx: 1` and `printBorderPt: 0.5`. Ordinary material paragraphs are
separate 2em roles and do not inherit the supplied-starter indentation.
